Why it is viable

A focused product, not a generic course site.

This MVP translates the submission into a credible landing and conversion website for a specialised institution: narrow positioning, selective admissions, rigorous outputs, and employer-facing proof of competence.

Problem

Advanced STEM graduates often have strong mathematics and coding ability but lack a structured bridge into quantitative finance practice.

Solution

A cohort-based programme built around simulation, model implementation, research notes, peer critique, and portfolio-grade artifacts.

Proof

The website centers on one action: pilot applications, supported by a clear programme model and credibility signals rather than inflated startup language.

Core offer

The first programme can launch with four modules.

A viable first version should stay narrow: one flagship track, one small cohort, and a visible output standard.

01 — Foundations

Probability, stochastic thinking, market microstructure intuition, Python environment setup.

02 — Modelling

Time-series work, factor logic, derivative intuition, simulation and backtesting discipline.

03 — Verification

Reproducible notebooks, technical write-ups, peer review, and oral defense of assumptions.

04 — Deployment readiness

Project showcase, hiring dossier, and partner-facing evidence of practical competence.

Target segments

The website speaks to a narrow, serious audience.

Primary users

Engineering, mathematics, physics, computer science, and technically strong economics students who want a rigorous transition into quantitative finance.

Secondary buyers

University partners, boutique funds, and regulated financial employers looking for a more credible signal than lightweight online certificates.

Positioning line: QuantLab Institute is not another finance content platform. It is a selective bridge institution for advanced technical talent that needs measurable proof of quant competence.

Pilot cohort
